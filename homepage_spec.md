# Exploration Servers – Homepage Specification

## 1. Purpose

This website promotes a gameplay concept rather than a single server.

It presents **“Exploration Servers”** as a structured exploration-based Minecraft survival experience, and acts as a hub linking to servers that implement this model.

---

## 2. Positioning

The site is **experience-first, not server-first**.

### Core Idea

Exploration is turned into progression.

### Messaging Pillars

- The world matters again  
- Exploration is progression  
- Discovery has value  
- Travel leads to meaningful outcomes

---

## 3. Homepage Structure

### 3.1 Hero Section

**Title:**  

Exploration, Reimagined

**Subtext:**  

A different way to play Minecraft survival.  

A world where travel matters, discoveries persist, and exploration drives progression.

**CTAs:**

- Explore Servers  
- How It Works

---

### 3.2 The Shift (Problem Framing)

Short, concise statements:

- Worlds are vast, but forgettable  
- Structures are found once, then ignored  
- Progress comes from farming, not discovering

**Closing line:**  

Exploration exists. It just isn’t the reason you play.

---

### 3.3 What This Changes

Introduce **“Exploration Servers”**:

- Named regions structure the world  
- Discoveries are tracked and rewarded  
- Progression is tied to exploration

**Key line:**  

You don’t just travel. You uncover the world.

---

### 3.4 The Experience (Core Section)

Narrative walkthrough:

- Spawn into the world  
- Choose a direction  
- Enter a named region  
- Discover a village or structure  
- Receive rewards  
- Progress is tracked  
- Continue exploring

**Goal:**  

Show the gameplay loop, not features.

---

### 3.5 Core Systems

Keep concise and readable:

**Named World**  

Every region has identity.

**Discovery Progression**  

Regions, villages, structures tracked.

**Meaningful Rewards**  

XP, claim blocks, achievements.

**Persistent World**  

Discoveries accumulate over time.

---

### 3.6 Exploration Servers Section

List servers implementing the concept.

**Example:**

**Charidh**

- Short description  
- Key stats (regions, villages, structures)  
- Join button

---

### 3.7 Why This Exists

Short philosophical statement:

Minecraft doesn’t need new mechanics.  

It needs a reason to use the world.

Exploration Servers exist to make the world matter again.

---

### 3.8 Call to Action

Final prompts:

- View Servers  
- Join Discord

---

## 4. Tone Guidelines

- Clear and grounded  
- Slightly aspirational  
- Avoid hype language  
- Avoid generic SMP phrases

### Do NOT use:

- “Vanilla+ experience”  
- “Unique features”  
- “Community-driven”

---

## 5. Key Differentiation

Other sites: **server-first**  

This site: **experience-first**

**Primary question answered:**  

“Why play this way?”

---

## 6. Risks

- Too abstract → unclear gameplay  
- Too feature-heavy → loses concept  
- Too negative → alienates players

Always anchor messaging in:

- Real mechanics  
- Real rewards  
- Clear gameplay loop
